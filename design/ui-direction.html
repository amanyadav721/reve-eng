Rules

One thing at a time.Each screen has one hero (the scene and the Now card) and one ink button. Everything else is quieter.
Color lives in the art.Screens are peach, cream and ink. Saturated color comes from the scene, Laddoo and rewards.
Label only what matters.On the trail, only the current stop gets a label. The rest are icons; tap one to see it.
The sky tells the time.Morning is pale and gold, afternoon blue, golden hour peach, night indigo with lit windows.
Memory shows up as a reason.Laddoo explains a suggestion with what it remembers ("You loved…"), so you can see it learning.
Flat illustration, no outlines.Shapes, soft shading and grain, the same in scenes, Laddoo, dish cards and stamps.
